Diagnosis:
The q35 control reproduces the failure without Left Hand Control installed or
running. QMP recorded WATCHDOG action=reset, followed by SHUTDOWN guest=true,
reason=guest-reset. This identifies the reset mechanism as the emulated chipset
watchdog; it does not identify an upstream kernel/firmware bug or patch.
The second suspend returns immediately without a second QMP SUSPEND transition.
EHCI instead of xHCI, one vCPU and SMM disabled did not repair it. Disabling
ICH9-LPC.enable_tco avoids the reset but still fails the second S3; rejected as
an incomplete workaround. Original failed reports remain historical failures.
